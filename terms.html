2. What Luné is – and what it is not

Luné is an app for personally recording and reviewing your menstrual cycle. It shows statistical estimates based on the information you enter yourself.

Luné is not a medical device and is not intended for diagnosing, treating, alleviating or preventing disease. It does not replace medical advice. For health questions or symptoms, please consult a doctor.

The app is not suitable for contraception. Predictions about ovulation and fertile days are estimates and can deviate considerably – even with careful tracking. Do not rely on Luné for contraception.

5. Master password and recovery code

Your data is encrypted with a key derived from your master password. During setup you also receive a one-time recovery code.

Please note: we cannot reset your password and cannot restore your data. This is not a missing feature but the necessary consequence of nobody but you being able to decrypt the data. If you lose your password and your recovery code, the data is permanently lost.

Keep the recovery code in a safe place separate from your device. If you forget your password, you can unlock with the recovery code and then set a new password.
