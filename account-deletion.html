Luné stores your health data exclusively encrypted on your device. An account is optional and only used for the encrypted backup. This page explains how to delete your account and data completely – directly in the app, with no questions asked and no waiting period.

1. Deleting inside the app

Open Settings → My data → Delete all data. The app first removes the server copy (if you have an account), then all local data including the key material. If you have an account, the same step lets you choose to delete the account itself as well.

Deletion takes effect immediately and is final. There is no recovery – not even by us, because the server copy is end-to-end encrypted and unreadable to us.

2. Deleting without access to the app

If you no longer have the app installed or cannot access your account, send an email from the address the account was created with to: developer@berry-systems.de. We will delete the account with all server data and confirm the deletion to you.

3. What gets deleted

Deleting the account removes: the account email address, the sign-in credentials and the encrypted backup (ciphertext that is unreadable to us). There is no other server data – no analytics, no advertising, no profiles.

Data that only lives on your device is deleted via the in-app path or by uninstalling the app.
